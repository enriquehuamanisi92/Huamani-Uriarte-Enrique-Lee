# Synthetic Dataset Datasheet

## Motivation and composition

`comas_urban_crime_synthetic.csv` tests the code without exposing real information. It contains simulated zone-month observations, approximate coordinates, fictional territorial attributes, incident lags, and a derived future label. It contains no SIDPOL records, real complaints, victims, investigated persons, addresses, or operational municipal data. Sector names provide teaching context, but their values do not describe those sectors.

## Generation and provenance

The dataset is generated by `create_dataset.py` with NumPy and seed 42. Relationships were deliberately programmed; they cannot support inference about actual social associations. The CSV is reproducible and its DVC pointer identifies the version.

## Limitations and acceptable use

- It does not represent the real distributions, boundaries, or prevalence of Comas.
- It cannot validate substantive fairness or institutional utility.
- It must not be combined with personal data or presented as official statistics.
- It is suitable for execution tests, structure, version control, and classroom demonstration.

The future real dataset will receive a separate restricted datasheet covering provenance, legal basis, custody, variables, licensing, coverage, missingness, geocoding, transformations, access, retention, and disclosure review.
